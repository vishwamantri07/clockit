What is Distribution Shift?

Distribution shift occurs when incoming inspection images differ from the reference data used during model development or calibration.

It may happen because of lighting changes, camera differences, production variations or image quality.

Important Notice

Distribution shift is a monitoring signal, not proof of a product defect. The system should request review when necessary rather than automatically classify a product as defective.

Recommended Actions

  • Check camera position and image capture conditions.
  • Verify lighting consistency.
  • Review image quality and focus.
  • Compare incoming features with reference data.
  • Request human review before changing model settings.
